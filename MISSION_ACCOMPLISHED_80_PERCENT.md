## 💡 KEY INSIGHTS LEARNED

### **Root Cause Analysis**:
- Original "100% accuracy" was **completely fake** due to severe overfitting
- Model memorized specific people's faces, not exercise poses
- Only 20 original images caused catastrophic overfitting

### **Solution Strategy**:
- **Massive data collection** (6,110+ samples) solved overfitting
- **Quality filtering** ensured good pose landmarks
- **Advanced architecture** (Transformer) handles complex patterns
- **Balanced categories** prevent bias toward any exercise type

### **Production Lessons**:
- Always validate "perfect" results - they're usually overfitted
- Diverse data is more valuable than large amounts of similar data
- Quality processing pipelines are essential for real-world performance
- Comprehensive monitoring enables rapid iteration and debugging
