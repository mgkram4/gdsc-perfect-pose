From Space to Earth

Discover the everyday technologies developed from space exploration.

Technological Advancements

GPS Navigation

Originally developed for military and intelligence applications, the Global Positioning System (GPS) is now essential for navigation in cars, phones, and aircraft.

Camera Phones

NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory developed compact, low-power CMOS image sensors in the 1990s, paving the way for the cameras found in billions of smartphones today.

Scratch-Resistant Lenses

NASA needed a way to protect astronaut helmet visors from scratches. This led to the development of a special coating that is now widely used on eyeglasses.

Memory Foam

Developed to improve seat cushioning and crash protection for pilots and astronauts, memory foam is now a popular material in mattresses, pillows, and footwear.

CAT Scans

The digital image processing techniques NASA used to enhance images from the Moon were adapted to create Computer-Aided Tomography (CAT) and Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI).

Wireless Headsets

NASA developed hands-free communication systems for astronauts, which led to the wireless headsets used by millions for calls and music.
