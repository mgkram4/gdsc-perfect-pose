History of Space Exploration

A timeline of humanity's greatest achievements in space.

Major Milestones

1957: The First Satellite

The Soviet Union launches Sputnik 1, the first artificial satellite to orbit Earth, officially starting the space race.

USSR

1961: First Human in Space

Yuri Gagarin, a Soviet cosmonaut, becomes the first human to journey into outer space, completing one orbit of Earth.

USSR

1969: One Giant Leap for Mankind

The United States' Apollo 11 mission lands the first humans on the Moon. Neil Armstrong's first steps are watched by millions around the world.

USA

1998: The International Space Station

Assembly of the ISS begins, a collaborative project involving five space agencies: NASA (USA), Roscosmos (Russia), JAXA (Japan), ESA (Europe), and CSA (Canada).

International Collaboration

2015: Reusable Rockets

SpaceX, a private American company, successfully lands the first stage of its Falcon 9 rocket, a critical step towards reusable spacecraft and lower launch costs.

USA (Commercial)
